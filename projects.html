Recent Projects

Selected projects from WEC members that extend agricultural economics research to producers and communities across the western region.

Western Ranch Economics Decision Tool

Members: Author1, Author2 (University of Arizona)
Funded by: Western Extension Risk Management Education Center

An interactive budgeting tool designed to help western ranchers evaluate the economic viability of different stocking rates under variable forage conditions. The tool incorporates regional cost data from six western states and allows producers to model drought scenarios against their current operations.

Small Acreage Irrigation Cost-Benefit Analysis

Members: Jan Gonzales (UC Agriculture & Natural Resources)
Funded by: USDA NIFA

This project provides Extension educators with curriculum and worksheets for helping small-acreage producers in California and the Southwest evaluate the return on investment for upgrading from flood to drip irrigation systems, with particular attention to labor savings and water use efficiency.

Learn more →

Farm Succession Planning Workshop Series

Members: Multi-state collaboration
Funded by: Western Center for Risk Management Education

A six-state collaborative workshop series addressing the legal, financial, and interpersonal dimensions of farm and ranch succession. The project developed standardized materials that can be adapted to each state's legal framework while maintaining consistent economic guidance.
